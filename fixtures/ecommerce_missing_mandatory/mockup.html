How to read this mockup (C1/C5). One framed section per screen (9 screens, matching routes.json), then the component gallery. The dark bar above is mockup navigation, not app UI. Resize the window to see the real breakpoints: Mobile ≤375px (1-column grid, hamburger), Tablet 376–768px (2 columns, sidebar), Desktop ≥1024px (4 columns). All colours, spacing, radii and type resolve to tokens.json. Global interaction rules: hover darkens 8% (precomputed hexes #225BD8 / #B13428), click scales to 0.98, submitting buttons show a spinner and disable, success toasts auto-dismiss after 3s, modals fade + slide up over 200ms. Error messages shown below are byte-identical to validation-rules.json (NFR-007). Product data is the schema.sql seed.
