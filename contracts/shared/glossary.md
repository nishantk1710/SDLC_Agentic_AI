# Glossary

- **Cart** — A transient collection of menu items a customer intends to order from one restaurant.
- **COD** — Cash on Delivery — payment collected in person at the time of delivery.
- **Delivery Agent** — A courier who transports orders from restaurants to customers.
- **ETA** — Estimated Time of Arrival for a delivery.
- **PCI DSS** — Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard governing the handling of card data.
- **RBAC** — Role-Based Access Control.
- **SRS** — Software Requirements Specification (this document).
- **Tokenization** — Replacement of card data with a non-sensitive reference token held by the payment gateway.
